Hi, I'm Suchir — an Electronics and Communication Engineering student currently in my 7th semester. This site is where I document the projects I build outside the classroom, mostly centered around RF, embedded systems, and IoT.

## What I work on

I'm drawn to the space where hardware meets signal — antenna design, microwave engineering and embedded systems. Over the past few semesters I've built a foundation in:

- **Antenna design** 
- **Embedded systems**, including microcontroller programming and sensor integration
- **PCB design** — schematic design, placement and routing 
- **Signal processing**, with a focus on the theory behind microwave and communication systems


## Why this site exists

A resume can only show so much — a line of text doesn't capture the debugging, the redesigns, or the small decisions that actually teach you something. This portfolio is where I go deeper: each project post here contains more details about each project, the components and tools involved and showcases what I've learnt from it.
